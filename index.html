About VY-LIB

A youth-led movement for sustainable community impact.

“To cultivate a movement of empowered, active youth who drive sustainable development, environmental resilience, and positive social change across Liberia and beyond.”

Voluntary Youth – Liberia (VY-LIB) mobilizes young people across Liberia to address critical community needs through inclusive volunteerism, environmental stewardship, social service, and professional skill development.

Our Mission

“To mobilize and unite young people through inclusive volunteerism—fostering community-led solutions in environmental stewardship, social welfare, and youth capacity building to create equitable and resilient communities.”

Our Operational Pillars

Practical action areas for youth and communities.

🌳 Green Action & Climate

Environmental management, sanitation, tree planting and recycling.

12-month targets: 10 community cleanups, 1,000 trees planted and 5 school eco-clubs.

🤲 Social & Community

Education, public health and support for vulnerable groups.

12-month targets: 500 students taught, 4 supply drives and 6 health outreaches.

💻 Digital & Skills

Youth capacity building, career readiness and literacy.

12-month targets: 200 youth trained, a 50% placement rate and 4 career seminars.
